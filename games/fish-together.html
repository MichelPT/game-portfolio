Fish Together

What if your phone actually felt like the fishing rod?

CompletedJul 12, 2026Unity 6

Design notes

The phone becomes the rod

Fish Together is a multiplayer fishing game built around physical casting motion and haptic bite feedback. The interaction makes catching a fish something the player performs and feels, not just something they select.

A complete build in 20 minutes

Each match takes place in a temporary shared session called a Pond. For up to 20 minutes, players fish side by side, compete on a live leaderboard, and level up through catches.

Each level offers temporary buffs that can compound into a powerful build. When the Pond ends, every build disappears. The reset keeps the next match fair and makes every choice in the current run matter.

The core loop

Cast → catch → level up → compound → repeat. Each catch advances both the immediate competition and the player’s temporary strategy for that Pond.
