Design practice

How I work

01

Prototype the question

I build the smallest useful test, then use play and data to decide what survives.

02

Watch what players do

Hesitation, missed feedback, and ignored choices tell me what the next version needs.

03

Explain the system

I connect mechanics, player behaviour, and intended experience so decisions can be evaluated.
