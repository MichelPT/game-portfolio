Matakba

Android · June 2025 · University capstone

An Android app that reads ancient Batak script through the camera and transliterates it. It recognises more than 160 characters and averages under 30 ms per frame on lower-end phones. I trained a YOLOv11s model, added cross-attention, and converted it from PyTorch to NCNN to run on the device.

My part
Data annotation, model training, on-device inference
Built with
YOLOv11s, PyTorch, NCNN, C++, Android NDK
