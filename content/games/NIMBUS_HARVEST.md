# Nimbus Harvest

## Project facts

- **Date:** May 25, 2026
- **Status:** Released on the App Store; one post-launch patch; available in the European Union
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/id/app/nimbus-harvest/id6770053044
- **Roles:** Game designer, UI designer and implementer, gameplay polish

## Design

A cozy pixel-art farming game driven by the player’s local weather forecast. Sun, clouds, rain, snow, wind, and heat affect crop growth. Different crops prefer different conditions, turning the real forecast into a planning system.

## Interaction polish

- Earlier versions gave planting and harvesting little feedback and harvested crops automatically.
- Removed automatic harvesting so the player performs the action.
- Added animation, sound effects, and coin audio to make planting and harvesting more interactive, readable, and satisfying.
