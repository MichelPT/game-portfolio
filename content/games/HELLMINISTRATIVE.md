- **Date:** July 4, 2026
- **Event:** Game Seed 2026

- **Contributions:** Created and implemented the 3D table; illustrated and implemented the CAPTCHA content

## Design

A typing game about processing the Devil’s administrative requests. Time and accuracy affect an overheat bar that determines whether the level is won or lost. CAPTCHA events increase overheat 1.5× faster until resolved. Documents carry different priority weights and expire when ignored.

## Public positioning

Use this project as evidence of asset creation and implementation inside a larger gameplay system, plus understanding of layered pressure, prioritisation, and readable failure states.
