# Fih Together

The spelling **Fih Together** is intentional and must not be corrected to “Fish Together.”

## Project facts

- **Date:** July 12, 2026
- **Team:** Five people
- **Engine:** Unity 6
- **Primary language for Unity work:** C#
- **Roles:** Game designer, 3D artist, technical artist
- **Art and technical-art work:** Created the 3D environment, pixelated shaders, and dynamic pixelated water animation

## Design

A multiplayer fishing game where the phone acts as the rod through physical casting motion and haptic bite feedback. Matches take place in temporary shared sessions called Ponds, lasting up to 20 minutes. Players catch fish, level up, select temporary buffs, and compete on a live leaderboard. Progress resets after each Pond.

Core loop: **cast → catch → level up → compound → repeat.**

## Playtest

- **Participants:** 3
- **Main question raised:** Why does the game need a virtual Pond or other people fishing together?
- **Observed response:** Players looked forward to the social presence and fellowship of fishing together, but the multiplayer purpose needed to be made more meaningful.
- **Design response:** Implement Pond sessions that one player can create and others can join. As more players join, the group receives a greater chance of catching better fish.

## Public positioning

Use this project as evidence of multiplayer systems thinking, social motivation, Unity/C# familiarity, 3D environment work, shaders, and technical art.
