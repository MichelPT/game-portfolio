### Fish Together

- **Date:** July 12, 2026
- **Engine:** Unity 6
- Multiplayer fishing game where the phone functions as the rod.
- Uses physical casting motion and haptic bite feedback.
- Matches take place in temporary shared sessions called Ponds, lasting up to 20 minutes.

### Alcherogue

- **Date:** October 8, 2026
- Alchemy-shop game about pouring by eye without numerical measurements.
- Liquid colour is derived from mixture properties and acts as the player’s instrument.
- Accelerating flow makes large pours increasingly difficult to control.
- Potions use a quality ladder rather than binary success or failure.
- Rent, stock, haggling, and auctions repeat the same risk decision at different scales.
- The game was rebuilt twice after structural testing invalidated earlier concepts.
- A Python script proved every four-jar deduction arrangement could be solved in exactly two pours with one fixed strategy.
- Economy scripts exposed excessive free stock, predictable shortages, and rent pressure that arrived too late.
- Required precision varied by 3.1× between recipes; visible hue feedback varied by 4.8×.
- One recipe produced only 2.8° of visible hue change.
- A browser prototype compared constant and accelerating flow.
- Methods included MDA, game-feel analysis, perceptual testing, simulation, and comparative analysis.
